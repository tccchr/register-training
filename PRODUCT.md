# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Employees use the portal to review available training courses, register for classes, view their learning calendar, and manage their own booking status. Admins and approvers use the same system to create courses, manage classes, assign employees, review rosters, and keep registration data accurate.

## Product Purpose

Register Training is an internal training registration portal for TCCC Learning LAB. Its job is to make course discovery, class booking, approval, and roster management clear enough that employees can act without asking HR or admins for status updates. Success means users quickly understand what is open, what requires attention, and what action is available next.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, premium. The interface should borrow Apple's restraint and product focus, while staying grounded in TCCC Learning LAB identity: confident blue, bright yellow as a brand cue, and clean Thai-first typography.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI dashboard patterns: decorative gradients, nested cards, heavy shadows, noisy color palettes, oversized chrome, and repeated badges that do not carry status. Avoid making the training portal feel like a marketing landing page; the design should support fast operational decisions.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the primary action obvious on every screen.
2. Use status only when it changes what the user should do next.
3. Let spacing and alignment create hierarchy before adding visual decoration.
4. Treat Thai readability as a first-class design requirement.
5. Keep motion short, purposeful, and respectful of reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Preserve keyboard focus indicators, minimum touch targets around 44px where possible, reduced-motion fallbacks, and Thai text line heights that remain readable on desktop and mobile.
